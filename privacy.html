Privacy Policy - Fit Calculator

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Welcome to Fit Calculator, available at fitcalculator.site. Fit Calculator is a free fitness calculator website with tools such as a BMI calculator, BMR calculator, body fat calculator, water intake estimator, healthy weight range estimator and daily calorie calculator.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, what we do not collect, and how we use it. By using Fit Calculator, you agree to this policy. Please also read our Terms and Conditions and Disclaimer.

Quick Summary

  • We do not store the health details you enter in our calculators.
  • You do not need to create an account to use Fit Calculator.
  • We use Google AdSense to show ads and Google Analytics to understand website traffic.
  • These services use cookies. You can control or opt out of them.

1. Information We Collect

Health data you enter in calculators: NOT collected

When you use our health calculator tools, you may enter details such as height, weight, age, gender, activity level, or body measurements.

  • All calculations happen in your browser on your own device.
  • This information is not sent to our server and is not saved or stored by us.
  • When you close or refresh the page, the values you entered are gone.
  • We cannot see your inputs or results, and we cannot share or sell them.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, Fit Calculator and our service providers may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, date and time of visit, and referring website.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, screen size, and language settings.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: small files or identifiers stored on your browser or device (see Section 5).

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, address, or payment details.
  • We do not collect your medical records or health history.
  • We do not collect your precise location.

If you contact us by email or through our Contact Us page, we will receive your email address and the message you send. We use it only to reply to you.

2. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used to:

  • Run, maintain, and improve Fit Calculator and our free calculators.
  • Understand which tools and pages are most useful to visitors.
  • Keep the website secure and prevent spam, abuse, and invalid ad clicks.
  • Show ads through Google AdSense, which helps us keep our tools free.
  • Respond to your questions and feedback.
  • Follow legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone for money.

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

Fit Calculator uses Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to display ads on our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to Fit Calculator and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie to serve ads on websites in its network. DART helps show relevant ads and limit how often you see the same ad.
  • We do not have access to these cookies, and we do not control how advertisers or Google use them.

Opting out of personalized ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising in these ways:

  • Visit Google's Ads Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads.
  • Visit https://www.aboutads.info to opt out of interest-based ads from participating third-party vendors.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data on its partner sites page: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to your interests.

Visitors from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before using cookies for personalized advertising. You can accept, reject, or change your choice at any time using the cookie consent tool on our website.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use FitCalc, such as which calculators are popular, how long people stay, and which country or device they use.

  • Google Analytics uses cookies and collects usage data like pages viewed, approximate location, browser, and device type.
  • This data is used for statistics and website improvement only.
  • Google Analytics does not receive the health values you enter in our calculators, because those stay in your browser.

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Cookies and How Users Can Control Them

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Fit Calculator and its partners may use:

  • Essential cookies: help the website work properly.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand website usage.
  • Advertising cookies: used by Google and its partners to show and measure ads.

How to control cookies

  • Change your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Check the Help section of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or your browser of choice.
  • Use the cookie consent options on our website, where available.
  • Use the opt-out links in Sections 3 and 4.

If you block all cookies, some parts of the website may not work as expected.

Please read the privacy policy of any site you visit. The ads on our website do not mean that we endorse the products or services advertised.

7. Children's Privacy

FitCalc is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us through our Contact Us page and we will delete it promptly. Users under 18 should use Fit Calculator with the guidance of a parent or guardian.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we handle, including serving our website over a secure HTTPS connection. Because the health values you enter are not stored on our server, they cannot be leaked from our systems.

However, no website or internet transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Whatever country you live in, you can ask us to:

  • Access: tell you what personal information we hold about you, if any.
  • Delete: remove any personal information we hold, such as emails you sent us.
  • Opt out: stop personalized ads and analytics tracking using the tools in Sections 3, 4, and 5.

GDPR: users in the EU, EEA, and UK

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you also have the right to correct your data, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority. We process data based on your consent (for cookies and personalized ads) and our legitimate interest in running and securing the website.

CCPA: California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), California residents may request to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising cookies used by Google may count as "sharing" under California law, and you can opt out using Google's Ads Settings or by contacting us. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Users in India

We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Much of the advertising and analytics data is held by Google, not by us. To manage that data, please use Google's own privacy tools. To exercise any right with us, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
