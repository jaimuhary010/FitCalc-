Pool Chlorine Basics

Simple answers to help you understand your pool water.

🏊

What Is Pool Chlorine?

Chlorine is a sanitiser added to pool water to kill bacteria and other germs and to break down contaminants.

💧

Why Is Free Chlorine Important?

Free chlorine is the active part that sanitises water. Too little leaves water unprotected, and too much can irritate skin and eyes.

🧪

Free vs Combined Chlorine

Free chlorine is available to sanitise. Combined chlorine has already reacted with contaminants and is much less effective.

🧮

How the Calculator Works

It finds the extra chlorine needed, converts it to grams of active chlorine, then divides by your product's strength.

☀️

What Affects Chlorine Levels

Sunlight, swimmers, rain, heat, debris and stabiliser levels all change how quickly chlorine is used up.

🔍

How Often to Test

Many pool owners test free chlorine regularly, often daily in busy season. Check your product and local guidance.

⚖️

Pool Water Balance

pH, alkalinity and stabiliser levels all affect how well chlorine works. Test and adjust them together.

Swimming Pool Chlorine Calculator: A Complete Guide to Pool Chlorine Levels and Dosage

What Is Swimming Pool Chlorine?

Swimming pool chlorine is a chemical sanitiser that keeps pool water clean and safe-looking. It destroys bacteria and algae and oxidises organic matter such as sweat, oils and leaves. Without it, pool water can quickly become cloudy and unhygienic.

What Is Free Chlorine?

Free chlorine is the portion of chlorine in the water that is still active and ready to sanitise. It is measured in parts per million (ppm) with a pool test kit. Once chlorine reacts with contaminants it becomes combined chlorine, which is far less useful. That is why the free chlorine level is the number most pool owners track.

How Does a Chlorine Calculator Work?

A Daily Chlorine Calculator estimates how much product is needed to raise your free chlorine from its current level to a target level. It uses four inputs: pool volume, current free chlorine, target free chlorine and the strength of your product. First it finds the increase needed in ppm, then converts that into grams of active chlorine, and finally divides by your product's available chlorine to give a Chlorine Dosage. A Pool Chlorine Calculator gives a theoretical figure, so it cannot replace the label.

How to Calculate Pool Water Volume

Accurate Pool Water Volume is the foundation of any dosage estimate. For a rectangular pool, multiply length by width by average depth. For a round pool, multiply pi by the radius squared, then by the average depth. Irregular pools are harder, so use a careful estimate or the volume given by the pool builder.

Factors That Affect Chlorine Levels

Chlorine does not last forever. Strong sunlight breaks it down, heavy bather loads use it up, and rainfall, leaves, sunscreen and other organic contaminants consume it. Warm water, poor circulation and the amount of stabiliser in the water can also change how much Free Chlorine Level you actually keep.

Why Should You Test Pool Water Regularly?

Calculators work from the numbers you give them, so reliable test results matter. Regular testing shows whether chlorine is too low to protect swimmers or too high for comfort. Testing again after treatment confirms the result and helps you spot problems early.

Important Chlorine Safety Tips

Always read and follow the product label. Wear gloves and eye protection, add chemicals as directed, and never mix chlorine products with each other or with acids, ammonia or cleaners, because dangerous gases can form. Keep products in their original containers, away from children and heat.
