We'd Love to Hear From You

Welcome to FitCalc, your free fitness calculator website at fitcalculator.site. Whether you have a question, a suggestion, or found a problem, we are happy to help.

FitCalc is run by real people who care about making simple health tools available to everyone. Please use the details below to contact us.

Why Contact Us?

You can write to us for any of these reasons:

  • Calculator Feedback: Tell us what you like and what we can improve.
  • Report a Bug or Wrong Result: Found something that looks incorrect? Let us know.
  • Suggest a New Calculator: Have an idea for a useful health or fitness tool?
  • Advertising or Business Inquiry: For partnership or business questions.
  • Privacy or Data Request: For questions about your privacy rights. See our Privacy Policy.
  • General Questions: Anything else about FitCalc.

How to Contact Us

The easiest way to reach us is by email:

Email: contact@fitcalculator.site

We usually reply within 24 to 48 hours on working days. If you do not hear back, please check your spam folder or send your message again.

Important: Please do not send personal medical reports, test results, or emergency health questions through email or the contact form. We cannot give medical advice. For any health concern or emergency, please consult a qualified doctor or contact your local emergency services immediately.

We will only use your name and email to reply to you. We do not sell or share them. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FitCalc free?

Yes. All our calculators are free to use, and you do not need to create an account. We show ads through Google AdSense to cover our costs.

Is my health data saved?

No. The details you enter, such as height, weight, and age, are used for calculation in your browser and are not saved on our server. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I suggest a new calculator?

Absolutely. Choose "Feedback" in the form or email us with your idea. We review all suggestions.

How do I report a wrong calculation?

Choose "Report an Issue" in the form or email us. Please tell us the calculator name, the values you entered, the result you received, and what you expected. This helps us check it quickly.
