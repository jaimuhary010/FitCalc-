How to Take Your Measurements

Use a flexible measuring tape, keep it snug without squeezing the skin, and measure the same way each time.

1

Height

Stand straight without shoes, heels together and back against a wall. Look straight ahead and mark the top of your head.

2

Neck

Measure just below the larynx (Adam's apple), with the tape sloping slightly down toward the front. Keep shoulders relaxed.

3

Waist

Measure around the waist at the level of your navel after a relaxed breath out. Keep the tape level and do not pull in your stomach.

4

Hips

For the female equation, measure around the widest part of the hips and buttocks, with feet together and the tape level.

  • Use a flexible tape, snug but not compressing the skin.
  • Measure bare skin or thin clothing, and take each measurement twice.
  • Use the same positions and time of day whenever you re-measure.

Body Fat Calculator: Understanding Body Fat Percentage by Measurements

What Is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the share of your total body weight that is fat. The rest is lean body mass, which includes muscle, bone, organs and water. Some fat is essential for hormone function, energy storage and protecting organs, so the goal is not zero fat.

How Does a Body Fat Calculator Work?

A Body Fat Calculator estimates your percentage from measurements instead of scanning your body. You enter simple numbers such as height and circumferences, and a formula turns them into an estimate. A Body Fat Estimator is convenient and free, but it is only as reliable as the measurements and the equation behind it.

What Is the U.S. Navy Body Fat Method?

The U.S. Navy Body Fat Calculator uses a published circumference-based method. It applies base-10 logarithms to height and to the difference between waist and neck, and for women it adds the hip measurement. Because the equations are sex-specific, there is a male and a female version.

Why Are Waist, Neck and Hip Measurements Important?

Fat stored around the middle tends to differ from fat stored elsewhere, so waist size is a strong clue. The neck acts as a rough guide to lean tissue in the upper body, and the hips reflect fat distribution, which is why they matter in the female equation. Together these allow Body Fat Percentage by Measurements to be estimated.

Difference Between Body Fat Mass and Lean Body Mass

Fat mass is the weight of fat in your body, and lean body mass is everything else. If you enter your weight, fat mass equals weight times body fat percentage divided by 100, and lean mass is the remainder. Both figures are estimates that depend on the percentage.

Body Fat Percentage Versus BMI

BMI compares weight with height and cannot tell muscle from fat. Body fat percentage tries to describe composition itself, so it can add context, especially for muscular or very sedentary people. Neither number is a diagnosis on its own.

Limitations of Circumference-Based Estimates

When you Calculate Body Fat Percentage this way, small measuring errors can shift the result noticeably. The method may also be less accurate for very lean or very heavy people, older adults, and body shapes that differ from the groups used to build the equations. Treat the result as a rough guide.

Other Methods of Measuring Body Composition

Other options include skinfold calipers, bioelectrical impedance scales, DEXA scans, hydrostatic weighing and air displacement plethysmography. These can be more precise but may need equipment or a clinic visit. A healthcare professional can advise which method suits you.
